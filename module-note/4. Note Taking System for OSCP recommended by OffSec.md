# Several Learning Methods

> [!note] NOTES
> ### **Cornell Note Taking** **Method**: 
> ![[cornell-note-example.png]]
> 
> ### Retrieval Practice: 
> Flashcards, write questions you come up along your studies on a flashcard to then quiz yourself in the future.
> 
> ### Spaced Learning: 
> Studying in a spaced out manner compared to cramming it just before the exam. 
> 
>### SQ3R: 
>stands for Survey, Question, Read, Recite, Review. You will start by reading headlines or overview of the chapter to get an understanding of what to come on a very high level. Next you would write questions based on those high level understanding on what you would like to be answered by the end of the module. You would then "Read" or watch or do the module start to end and then "Recite" the module by answering the questions you had written in the start, for ones that you forgot, you can "Review" the module.
>
>### Feynman Technique:
>Learn the module and then attempt to explain it in layman terms to a beginner or anyone not familiar with the topic. If you struggle then you can identify the gaps of understanding and review.


> [!question] CUES & QUESTIONS
>## Study Session Reflection
>- What time did I start the study session?
>- How long was the study session?
>- Did I get interrupted (if so, how did that happen)?
>- What did I do just before I started studying?
>- What did I eat or drink before I started studying?
>- What was my study location like? Was it quiet or busy?
>- What did I do during the study session specifically?
